# Mesajify marketing redesign — 6 November 2026

User correction supersedes the earlier request for identical VeriBurada design: retain craft and depth, create a distinct Mesajify identity, use independently created fictional brands/products, vary imagery between films, and add substantive supporting pages.

## Reference lock

- Mesajify's existing brand system is the authority: cobalt action/accent, near-black text, white/cool surfaces; green belongs to the logo and WhatsApp states. Use Outfit, not VeriBurada's Seed font.
- Loops (https://loops.so/) is the dominant structural reference: put a coherent product workflow in the foreground, then deepen into specific capabilities. Do not copy its content or its visual identity.
- Customer.io (https://customer.io/) supplies only the secondary information-architecture lesson: platform capabilities and audience-specific solutions deserve real detail pages. No borrowed metrics, customer logos, claims or features.
- VeriBurada supplies only the user's quality benchmark: authored, purposeful films, working mobile layouts, considered lower-level pages. Its pale-green atmosphere, capsule header, slogan and stacked service sequence are not the new target.

## Design decisions

- Center-led opening with a wide product film, quiet category label, concise business promise, two practical actions.
- Cobalt #2f5bff, ink #121a2d, cool canvas #f4f7fc, white #ffffff. A dark ink gallery may provide one strong contrast section. No ubiquitous blue/purple gradient blobs or generic glass cards.
- A rectangular, understated navigation bar with a real product menu and deep links; no copied morphing capsule.
- Three different fictional businesses: Novi Ev (kettle), Mori Atölye (ceramics), Rota Adım (footwear). Original simple source photos and new advertising designs. These are illustrative inputs and offers, never customer results.
- Discovery film retains the successful map/request/group story, with different example categories and the new palette. Creative film uses Mori, campaign film Novi, inbox film Rota. The overview joins those distinct examples; no single ad repeated everywhere.
- Reuse the visibility/reduced-motion/secure-message mechanics of the authored film engine, not the old page composition. New desktop and mobile exports must match the new scenes.

## Content depth

Home plus dedicated pages for Creative Studio, WhatsApp campaigns, shared inbox, business-list requests, examples, the complete workflow, solutions and meaningful solution detail pages. Each page needs specific content, a relevant visual, limitations grounded in the inspected application, related links and a clear next action.

Do not fabricate public prices, testimonials, delivery rates, order-management features, team assignment, payment integrations or guaranteed commercial results. Any prices in fictional advertisements are marked as illustrative demo data outside the advertisement.

Check all public links/routes, responsive overflow, navigation, example selectors/comparison controls, film controls, reduced motion, and video decoding.
